How to read this page
  • This page shows who the chosen app talks to over the internet: which servers, how much data, and when.
  • It only sees the outside of the traffic (addresses, sizes, timing) — never what is inside messages, because that is encrypted.
  • Alerts come from fixed rules, not from AI. Each one says how sure it is: observed = seen directly in the traffic, inferred = a conclusion drawn from what was seen.
  • An alert means unusual behavior worth reviewing — it is not proof that the app did anything wrong.
  • The risk score adds up the alerts: 0–39 low, 40–69 medium, 70+ high.
  • Hover or tap a ? to see what a technical word means.
